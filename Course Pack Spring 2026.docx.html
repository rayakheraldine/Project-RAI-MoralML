Dear student,

In the middle of April 2026 we are going to start an expedition of a couple of weeks into Responsible AI from multiple perspectives: Data Science, Law and Ethics. To ensure a safe and effective learning journey, we created an onboarding checklist. It is essential that you read it carefully and follow the instructions according to the deadlines. Please contact us if you have any questions or problems.
